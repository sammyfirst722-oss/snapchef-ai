**Q:** Can I bake salmon and asparagus together?  
**A:** Yes! Toss both with olive oil, salt, and pepper, then bake at 400°F for 12–15 minutes until the salmon flakes easily and the asparagus is tender-crisp.

**Q:** What seasonings pair best with salmon and asparagus?  
**A:** Lemon garlic, fresh dill, smoked paprika, or a butter-and-lemon glaze complement the natural flavors without overpowering them.

**Q:** Is salmon and asparagus a healthy dinner option?  
**A:** Absolutely. Salmon provides omega-3 fatty acids and high-quality protein, while asparagus is rich in fiber, vitamins A and C, and folate—making it a balanced, nutrient-dense meal.
