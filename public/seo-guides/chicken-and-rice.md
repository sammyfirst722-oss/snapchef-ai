## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the best way to cook chicken and rice together?**
A: Use a heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven. Sauté chicken first, then add rice and broth for even cooking and maximum flavor.

**Q: Can I make chicken and rice in a slow cooker?**
A: Yes! Brown chicken first, then combine with rice, broth, and seasonings. Cook on low for 6-8 hours or high for 3-4 hours.

**Q: How do I prevent my chicken and rice from getting mushy?**
A: Rinse rice before cooking, use the correct liquid ratio (usually 2:1), and avoid over-stirring once it's simmering.
