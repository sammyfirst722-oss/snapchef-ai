### 4. FAQ Schema (3 Questions)

**Q: What type of apple is best for cooking with pork?**
**A:** Firm, tart apples like **Granny Smith, Honeycrisp, or Pink Lady** hold their shape during roasting or sautéing and provide acidity to balance the fat.

**Q: What internal temperature should pork be cooked to?**
**A:** Cook pork chops, roasts, and tenderloin to **145°F (63°C)** followed by a 3-minute rest for juicy, safe results (ground pork requires 160°F).

**Q: Can I make pork and apple recipes in a slow cooker?**
**A:** Yes. Sear pork first for flavor, then cook on **LOW 6–8 hours** with sliced apples, onions, and cider; add apples in the last hour if you prefer texture over sauce.
